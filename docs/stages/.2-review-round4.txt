2. **[major] docs/stages/2-design-system.md:53-54, :61, :66.** `focus-on-rail` is a custom utility with no variant prefix. In Tailwind v4, utilities without a variant are emitted before variant utilities. Nuxt UI's button base has `focus:outline-none`, and the navigation-menu link has `focus-visible:outline-none`. Both have the same specificity and come later in the CSS, so they cancel the white outline. tailwind-merge can't resolve this, because it doesn't know the custom utility.
   - The rail then falls back to Nuxt UI's `focus-visible:before:ring-primary`: action-600 on civic-800 is **2.08** (needs 3).
